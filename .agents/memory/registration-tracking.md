---
name: Registration tracking
description: Product and trust boundaries for tracking students' external event registrations
---

Keep Registered as a separate list within Saved rather than adding another top-level navigation destination.

**Why:** The user asked for a recommendation between the two placements; grouping both personal event lists avoids adding to the mobile navigation.

**How to apply:** Preserve distinct Saved and Registered states, and allow a mistaken registration confirmation to be moved back to Saved.

Opening EngageSC or a registration link is not proof of registration. Ask for confirmation after the student returns, including to another SignalSC tab. Registration tracking stays browser-local like Saved unless a later request introduces accounts or an authorized registration-verification integration.

**Why:** External registration is completed outside SignalSC; the user's confirmation is the available evidence, not a verified response from EngageSC.

**How to apply:** Label registrations as self-reported, do not infer completion from clicks or time away, and keep decline, deferral, undo, and blocked-storage paths usable.
